OUR STORY & HERITAGE

Where Centuries of Royal Tradition Meet Culinary Mastery

Founded on the belief that dining is not merely sustenance but a sacred celebration of life, THE ROYAL PLATE recreates the lost grandeur of India’s royal banqueting halls.

Every creation on our menu is a masterwork born from sacred culinary archives. From slow-simmered Awadhi gravies in sealed clay vessels to Lucknowi kebabs that dissolve effortlessly on the palate, our master bawarchis (chefs) guard recipes handed down across royal generations. We use cold-pressed artisanal oils, pure Kashmiri saffron, and organic stone-ground spices.

Dum Pukht Cooking

Slow cooking in dough-sealed degchis preserving delicate natural aromatics.

Heirloom Royal Spices

Handpicked saffron from Pampore, Malabar cardamom, and stone-ground masalas.

Imperial Hospitality

Attentive, bespoke service ensuring every guest is treated as sovereign royalty.

Regal Ambience
